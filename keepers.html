KEEPER LAW

The keeper constitution

Two rounds earlier. Two seasons max. No waiver romances.

Drafted or traded-for players can be kept. Waiver pickups cannot. Nobody originally drafted in Rounds 1–3 can be kept. Cost moves two rounds earlier every keeper year, and after two keeper seasons the relationship is over.
